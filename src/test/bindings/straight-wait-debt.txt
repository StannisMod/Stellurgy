# method by a read is a settle: spend N ticks, then look. It is a wait on a state as much as a loop is, so a
# @Wait of its method names its line (a WINDOW, an EXPERIMENT), or it becomes a link. The list only shrinks: a
# method with more unnamed settles than its line says fails, one with fewer fails until its line is lowered.
